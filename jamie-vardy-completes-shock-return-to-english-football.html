Jamie Vardy Completes Shock Return to English Football with Burnley Move

2026-09-06 3 min read

Jamie Vardy is back in English football. The veteran striker has completed a surprise move to Burnley on a one-year deal, returning to the Championship after a brief spell in Italy.

The 39-year-old was a free agent following his departure from Cremonese, who were relegated from Serie A at the end of last season. The move marks a dramatic return to English shores for Vardy and his wife Rebekah, who had relocated to Italy for his first venture abroad.

Struggling Clarets Turn to Veteran Experience

Burnley's need for Vardy's services is clear. Relegated from the Premier League last term, the Clarets currently sit bottom of the Championship table, without a win from their opening five fixtures.

The club has recorded three draws and two defeats, scoring six goals while conceding twelve. They are desperate for the firepower and leadership that Vardy can provide.

Iconic Leicester Legacy

The former England international cemented his legendary status during an iconic spell of more than a decade at Leicester City. He scored 200 goals in 500 appearances for the Foxes, lifting the Premier League, FA Cup, and Community Shield.

Vardy famously rose from non-League football to star in Leicester's miraculous 2015-16 Premier League title triumph, while also earning 26 England caps.

Proved His Worth in Italy

Following Leicester's relegation from the Premier League at the end of the 2024-25 season, Vardy tested himself abroad for the first time in his career. During his stay in Serie A with Cremonese, he found the net seven times in 28 appearances, proving his goalscoring instinct remains intact.

'A Proper Footballing History'

Speaking after completing his move to Turf Moor, Vardy expressed his enthusiasm for the challenge ahead.

"I'm really happy to now be here at Burnley," Vardy said in a club statement.

"This club has a proper footballing history and passionate fans who deserve a team that represents them and their town," he added.

"I can't wait to get going on the pitch and help the team move in the right direction," he concluded.

Competition for His Signature

Burnley fended off interest from several clubs to secure Vardy's signature. According to reports, he had turned down offers from MLS, Serie A, and even the Premier League.

Sheffield Wednesday, the club Vardy supported as a boy, had also expressed a keen interest in bringing him to Hillsborough. However, the Owls' attempts to sign the striker were thwarted by EFL restrictions, paving the way for Burnley to complete the deal.

A Mission to Turn the Season Around

Burnley head coach Nicky Hayen is excited about what his new striker will add to his struggling squad.

"We are delighted to welcome Jamie to Burnley," Hayen said.

"His experience and leadership will be invaluable to our squad, and we believe he can make a significant impact on our campaign. Jamie has a proven track record, and we are looking forward to seeing him in a Claret shirt," he added.

The deal has an option for a further year if Burnley are promoted, highlighting the immediate impact the club hopes Vardy will bring to their promotion push.
